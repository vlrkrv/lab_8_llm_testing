Human space exploration began in 1957 with the launch of the satellite Sputnik 1. Just twelve years later, astronauts landed on the Moon for the first time, a milestone for all of humanity. Today, the International Space Station orbits Earth and serves as a laboratory for research in microgravity. Robotic rovers and probes continue to explore Mars, the outer planets, and even interstellar space. In the future, new technologies may allow people to build bases on the Moon and travel to Mars.
