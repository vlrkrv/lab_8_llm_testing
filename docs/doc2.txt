Our Solar System formed roughly 4.6 billion years ago from a giant cloud of gas and dust. At its center lies the Sun, which holds eight planets, dozens of moons, and countless asteroids and comets in its gravitational grip. The inner planets, such as Earth and Mars, are rocky, while giants like Jupiter and Saturn are made mostly of gas. Earth is the only known world that supports life, thanks to its liquid water and protective atmosphere. Studying neighboring planets helps researchers better understand how our own planet evolved.
